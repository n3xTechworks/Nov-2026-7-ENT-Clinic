3. Appointments

  • Online bookings are confirmed by SMS and email. Please arrive 15 minutes early for registration and bring any previous reports and a photo ID.
  • Appointment times are estimates. Emergencies and procedures can cause delays, and we will keep you informed.
  • You can reschedule or cancel through the patient portal or by calling at least 2 hours before your slot.
  • If we need to change your appointment, we will contact you and offer the next available slot.

4. Fees, deposits and refunds

Consultation fees are shown when you book and are usually paid at the clinic. Some procedures and tele-consultations require an advance deposit by UPI or card. Deposits are refunded in full for cancellations made more than 24 hours before the appointment, and refunds reach your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Insurance coverage depends on your policy and insurer; we will help with paperwork but cannot guarantee approval.
