Our story

Specialist ENT care, without the runaround

Tridya began as a two-room ENT practice with one simple idea: a patient with a blocked ear, a hoarse voice or a child who snores should be examined, tested and given a clear plan in a single visit.

Eighteen years later we are a team of five ENT surgeons and a lead audiologist, each running a focused clinic for hearing, sinus, voice, head and neck, and children's ENT. Diagnostics such as video endoscopy, hearing tests and sleep studies sit on the same floors as the consulting rooms, so results are ready while you are still with your doctor.

Milestones

How Tridya grew

Each new clinic started with a specialist who wanted patients to stop travelling to Chennai for ENT care.

  1. 2008

    The clinic opens

    Two consulting rooms and an endoscopy suite on Race Course Road, with the city's first walk-in ENT evening OPD.

  2. 2014

    Hearing & Balance programme

    Dr. Meera Raghavan sets up the hearing and balance lab and a newborn hearing screening service.

  3. 2016

    Day-care sinus surgery

    Dr. Arjun Venkatesh introduces day-care FESS, so most sinus patients go home the same evening.

  4. 2017

    One-stop neck lump clinic

    Dr. Farhana Iqbal starts a clinic where scan, needle test and specialist opinion happen in one visit.

  5. 2018

    Children's ENT service

    Dr. Rahul Menon builds a child-friendly service with play audiometry and a dedicated paediatric list.

  6. 2019

    Voice & Swallowing Clinic

    Dr. Kavitha Sundaram brings video stroboscopy to Coimbatore for singers, teachers and other voice users.

  7. 2025

    Patient portal and video consults

    Reports, prescriptions and bookings move online, and follow-ups can happen by video from home.
