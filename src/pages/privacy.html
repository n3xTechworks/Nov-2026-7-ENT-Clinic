This policy applies to our website, online booking, patient portal and the care you receive at the clinic.

1. Who we are

Tridya ENT Clinic ("Tridya", "we", "us") is a specialist ear, nose and throat clinic at 42 Race Course Road, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641018. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, book an appointment, use the patient portal or receive care at the clinic. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable Indian healthcare regulations.

2. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, age, gender, mobile number, email address and postal address.
  • Health information: symptoms, medical history, examination findings, endoscopy images and videos, audiograms, prescriptions, reports and treatment notes.
  • Appointment and billing details: booking history, insurance or TPA details, and payment records. We do not store full card numbers; payments are handled by our payment partner.
  • Family members: details of dependants you add to your patient account, such as children or parents.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, collected through essential and, with your consent, analytics cookies.

3. How we use your information

  • To book, confirm, remind you of and manage appointments.
  • To diagnose, treat and follow up your ENT condition, and to share results with you.
  • To process insurance claims and payments.
  • To reply to enquiries sent through our contact form, phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To meet legal, clinical and regulatory record-keeping requirements.
  • To improve our services, using anonymised or aggregated data wherever possible.

We never sell your personal information, and we do not use your health information for marketing.

4. Consent and your choices

By booking an appointment or submitting a form, you consent to us using your information for the purposes above. You may withdraw consent for SMS or WhatsApp reminders and non-essential cookies at any time from your patient profile or the cookie preferences panel. Withdrawing consent does not affect care you have already received, but some services, such as online booking, need basic contact details to work.

5. Who we share information with

  • Clinicians and staff at Tridya directly involved in your care, on a need-to-know basis.
  • Laboratories, imaging centres and hospitals we refer you to, with your knowledge.
  • Your insurer or TPA, when you choose cashless treatment or claim reimbursement.
  • Trusted service providers (appointment messaging, secure hosting, payments) bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Government or legal authorities, where the law requires it.

6. How we protect and keep your data

Medical records are stored on secure, access-controlled systems with encryption in transit and at rest. Staff access is logged and limited by role. We keep medical records for at least three years after your last visit, or longer where the law or your ongoing care requires, and then delete or anonymise them securely.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or update your personal data, request a copy of your medical records, ask us to erase data we no longer need to keep, or nominate someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to. Requests are answered within 30 days.

8. Children

We treat children of all ages. Information about patients under 18 is provided and managed by a parent or legal guardian, who may exercise the rights above on the child's behalf.

9. Contact our grievance officer

For any privacy question or complaint, write to our Grievance Officer at care@tridyaent.in, call +91 422 245 6780, or write to Tridya ENT Clinic, 42 Race Course Road, Coimbatore 641018. We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
