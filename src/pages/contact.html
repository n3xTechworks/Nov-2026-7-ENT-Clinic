FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call the front desk on +91 422 245 6780.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?{{icon plus}}

No. You can book any of our ENT specialists directly. Bring any previous reports or scans you have.

How long does a first visit take?{{icon plus}}

Plan for 45–60 minutes, including registration and any tests such as endoscopy or a hearing test.

Is there parking at the clinic?{{icon plus}}

Yes, free parking for 30 cars in the basement, with a lift to all floors.

Which insurance policies do you accept?{{icon plus}}

We are empanelled with 30+ insurers and TPAs for cashless day-care procedures. Call the desk with your policy number to check.

How do I get my reports?{{icon plus}}

Reports are uploaded to your patient portal the same day. You can also request printed copies at the front desk.

Can I reschedule or cancel online?{{icon plus}}

Yes. Log in to the patient portal and open Appointments, or call the desk at least 2 hours before your slot.
