Hearing care pathway

From hearing test to fitted hearing aid in three visits

A fixed, predictable plan, with every result stored in your portal.

Visit 1

Test and diagnose

  • Pure-tone audiometry and tympanometry
  • Consultation with the otologist
  • Audiogram uploaded to your portal
Visit 2

Trial

  • Choose from 3 hearing aid types
  • 7-day home trial
  • Daily-use log reviewed by audiologist
Visit 3

Fit and fine-tune

  • Final fitting and real-ear check
  • Phone app pairing
  • Follow-up at 1 and 6 months
