Dr. Rahul trained in Kochi and completed a paediatric ENT fellowship at a dedicated children's hospital. He joined Tridya in 2018 to build a child-friendly ENT service.

He treats glue ear, recurrent tonsillitis, adenoid problems and snoring in children, and works with the audiology team on newborn hearing screening.
