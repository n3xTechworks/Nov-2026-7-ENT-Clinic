Before you visit

Choosing the right specialist

Each consultant leads one clinic. If you are unsure, start with the symptom and we route you on the same day.

Hearing, ear or dizziness

Dr. Meera Raghavan, with Divya Prakash for hearing tests and hearing aids.

Sinus, allergy or snoring

Dr. Arjun Venkatesh runs the Sinus & Allergy Clinic and the sleep service.

Voice, swallowing or reflux

Dr. Kavitha Sundaram sees hoarseness, throat and swallowing problems.

Children and neck lumps

Dr. Rahul Menon for children; Dr. Farhana Iqbal for thyroid and neck lumps.
